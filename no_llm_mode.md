If LLMs were removed tomorrow, the intelligence that survives is judgment discipline.

This agent would still:
• default to restraint,
• prefer silence over explanation,
• avoid irreversible commitments,
• protect brand over efficiency,
• and refuse to act when confidence is low.

What disappears without LLMs is linguistic interpretation.
What remains is the posture:
do less,
wait longer,
and avoid harm.

The agent’s value is not prediction.
Its value is refusal.
